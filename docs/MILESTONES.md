# Milestones

## Milestone 0: protocol skeleton - complete

Core task, attempt, verifier, receipt, trajectory, settlement, worker, routing,
event, local store, and canonical digest behavior exists.

## Milestone 1: RL-grade local foundation - complete

The 0047-0074 queue added rights, privacy, quality validation, trace ingestion,
budgets, competition, human and probabilistic verification, router datasets,
scorecards, pilot packaging, reproducibility, cross-platform fixtures, risk gates,
retention, runtime and product boundaries, schema compatibility, snapshots,
performance smoke tests, and precise user-facing output.

Exit evidence:

- the funded RL-grade CLI walkthrough completes without network or credentials;
- golden snapshots and digest tests pass;
- local performance smoke covers more than 2,000 generated records;
- training export enforces quality, consent, rights, redaction, and withdrawal.

## Milestone 2: human-approved external dry run - next

Target: Hermes Agent issue #61631, subject to a fresh upstream check and maintainer
permission.

Exit criteria:

- explicit human risk approval and upstream contribution approval;
- focused contract and verifier policy accepted by the operator;
- real local attempt manifest and redacted trace captured without private prompts;
- authoritative verifier receipt for accepted or rejected work;
- human review receipt and review-friction notes;
- trajectory quality, cost, latency, and consent decision recorded;
- no real money, no production credentials, and no autonomous publication;
- post-pilot decision records whether to continue, revise, or stop.

## Milestone 3: read-only GitHub App pilot - planned

Exit criteria:

- least-privilege installation on a test repository;
- verified webhooks, durable delivery journal, replay, rate limits, and recovery;
- issue/PR/check ingestion into canonical local records;
- no comments, checks, labels, or settlement writes without separate approval;
- documented uninstall, data export, retention, and incident ownership.

## Milestone 4: limited funded pilot - blocked

Blocked on runner isolation, legal review, identity and dispute policy, real funding
and payment adapter review, operational support, and successful earlier pilots.

## Milestone 5: first learning experiment - blocked

Start with offline attempt-quality or router evaluation, not autonomous model control.
Blocked on sufficient diverse consented data, verifier calibration, leakage and
deduplication review, withdrawal propagation, experiment governance, and human
approval of the final dataset manifest.
